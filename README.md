Flexpy
===

## Inspiration
You have many apps on your mobile and computer that solve your needs. However, none know the current you as well as ChatGPT would.

You give your LLM chat provider so much current and incredibly personal context of your preferences, goals and background.

What if you could bring that personalisation across to any possible need you might have on-the-fly?

Additionally, people have so many unique and infrequent needs that don't and might never be exist on ChatGPT apps.

What if we could make the solution as and when you need it?

## What it does
We present, Flexpy, an mcp-app engine that creates mcp-apps on-the-fly to serve your needs no matter how common or niche – with the added benefits of bringing in your model context, and collaborative possibility.

Whether its something as common as travel planning with friends – where you can pull input from your group, with each getting personalised aid from their own unique contexts.

Or something niche like making a trivia game only your friends would find funny.

Flexpy does it!
